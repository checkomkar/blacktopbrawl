# Third-party models

Both models were downloaded from Sketchfab and converted with the scripts in `tools/`
(simplified, re-coloured, logo decals and badges removed from the bike).

- **Bike** (`bike.json`, `bike.bin`): based on "BMW S1000 RR"
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bmw-s1000-rr-1873aed292d6465694119bd81860a0c2)
  by VTX (https://sketchfab.com/VTX_car), licensed under CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0
  (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
  **Non-commercial only**; this modified version is under the same licence.
- **Rider** (`rider.gltf`, `rider.bin`, `rider.jpg`): based on "Racer Bike/Car"
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/racer-bikecar-9a7eae7771f94572aa72ca153153f340)
  by Subhash5922j (https://sketchfab.com/Subhash5922j), licensed under CC-BY-4.0
  (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
